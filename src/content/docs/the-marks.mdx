<MarkLegend client:visible />

The one people trip on is 📘. A closed record is **a statement of worth, not
an apology**: the project is telling you its deliberations are valuable
enough to keep — and honest enough to say so. Hiding that a record exists
would be a different (and worse) choice; the mark exists precisely so keeping
and disclosing can both be features.

The closed book is **blue** on purpose: The record's own ink, not a warning
color. A status mark that pleaded guilty would re-apologize the thing the
whole practice refuses to apologize for.
